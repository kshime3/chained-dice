🎲 3D dice: pick a style

Three ways the dice could look and move in Chained Dice. Press Roll on each (or roll them all together) and tell me which you like. Nothing in the game changes until you pick.

A. Spin in place

Real 3D cubes that hop and tumble where the dice sit today, then land on the rolled number.

  • No library: plain CSS, works by double-clicking index.html
  • Light: smooth on any phone
  • Closest to the current layout
  • Less "throw" feeling

B. Thrown across the felt

The same 3D cubes, but they fly in from the side, bounce, skid and settle, like a real throw.

  • No library: plain CSS, works offline
  • Light: smooth on any phone
  • More drama on every roll
  • Needs a little more room on the table

C. Lit 3D with shadows

Real lighting, shadows and a felt table, drawn with the Three.js 3D library. The dice tumble across and settle.
