Shift 1 music: darker and creepier

You wake up chained to the table. Which track plays for that first shift? Tap play on each and tell me the letter you like. Shifts 2 to 4 keep the current calm playlist, and the tense music still starts at shift 5. These stream from incompetech.com, so they need internet.

A. Ossuary 6 - Air

4:10 · very slow

A low drone that slowly builds a sense of doom. Barely any melody, mostly dread. The most "cellar" of the bunch.

B. Phantasm

3:39 · slow

Very deep, uneasy bass with distant bells. Feels like something is watching you from the dark.

C. Blue Feather

4:38 · very slow

Lonely, sad solo piano. Less "horror", more "hopeless". Quiet enough to sit under the dice sounds.

D. Spider Eyes

3:35 · no beat

An eerie soundscape: scratches, hums and odd noises. The creepiest, but not very musical.

E. Metaphysik

4:38 · slow

Strange, droning and dreamlike, like you're still half-asleep from whatever knocked you out.

F. This House

4:29 · slow

Creepy in a playful, haunted-house way. A bit of a wink, which might suit a casino that's out to get you.
