Card art: three styles

Four sample cards (one per theme), drawn in each style. Each style also shows how the small art looks on a hand tile. Pick one style (or mix: "A, but with C's boldness").

A. Dark engraved

Same look as the logo and crew portraits: dark panel, lamp-lit edges, rust, bone and a little blood red.

B. Old tarot card

Aged parchment, a thin ornate frame and a roman numeral, drawn in sepia ink. Feels like a collectible deck.

C. Bold flat icon

One big high-contrast symbol on the theme's color. Easiest to read at small sizes on phones.
