# Closed test invites (drafts for the owner)

Google needs **12+ testers opted in for 14 days in a row** before the game can go public. Aim for 15-20 people.
Fill in the [BRACKETS] once the closed test exists in the Play Console:
- **[OPT-IN LINK]**: Play Console > Test and release > Closed testing > your track > Testers > "Join on the web" link.
- **[PROMO CODE]**: Play Console > Monetize > Promo codes (one code per tester; the game is paid, so testers need one to get it free).
- **[START DATE] / [END DATE]**: the 14 days, counted from when most people have joined.
