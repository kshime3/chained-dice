## Full description (max 4,000)

```
You wake up chained to a craps table with your crew. The dealer wants his money.

Pay the debt each shift and he breaks one link of your 8-link chain. Break all eight and you walk out free. Come up short and the floor opens beneath you.

Chained Dice is a roguelike built on craps. Every run is different: new crew, new cards, new house rules, and dice that change as you play.

BET YOUR WAY OUT
- Real craps rules, explained as you play: a guided first shooter, live hints on every bet, and plain-English results.
- Pass Line or the dark side: back the shooter, or bet against your own crew.
- Odds, Lay Odds, Field, Place bets, Hard Ways, Come, Don't Come and the Fire Bet unlock as you go.

BUILD A RUN
- 58 cards across six themes: Dark side, Pairs, Hot streak, Money, Crew and Endless. Stack a theme and watch it grow.
- 11 one-use comps, from No Sevens to Double Down.
- Dice crafting: chisel, nudge and copy the faces of your own dice.
- Mult that builds as the shooter heats up, and cards that multiply it again.

YOUR CREW
- 9 characters, each with their own loaded dice: Lefty has no sixes, Jinx sevens out too often, Big Mo rolls high.
- Hire new crew into empty seats, or drop someone through the trapdoor for good.
- Shooting order matters: some crew warm up the table, others leave it cold.

THE HOUSE PLAYS BACK
- House rules that change every shift from shift 3, and a boss rule on the final link.
- The Pit Boss calls after each shift you survive with a choice of perks.
- Pit boss challenges on your own turn, with rewards.

MANY RUNS TO BEAT
- 55 hidden unlocks: cards, comps, crew and bets you discover by playing.
- Reputation: every few unlocks earns a lasting bonus for future runs.
- Walk out free, then take Double or nothing: endless mode, where debts and wins climb into the billions.

A dark, lamp-lit casino cellar, a moody soundtrack, and a dealer with a few last words for you when the floor opens.

Simulated gambling only: no real money, nothing to buy inside the game, no ads, no account. Plays fully offline.
```
