Chained Dice: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Chained Dice is a single-player game. It does not collect, send or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your run in progress, your unlocks and settings (sound, music volume, which tutorials you've seen) are saved only on your own device, in the game's local storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser's site data, deletes them.

What the game does not do

No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases, and no internet connection is needed to play. The game asks for no permissions on your device.

Feedback

The optional "Send feedback" note at the end of a run only copies text to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you choose to paste it into a message yourself.

Purchases

If you bought the game on Google Play, the purchase is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy. The game itself receives no payment details.

Children

Chained Dice is a simulated gambling game for adults. It uses no real money and offers no prizes, and it is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Use the developer contact email on the game's Google Play page.
